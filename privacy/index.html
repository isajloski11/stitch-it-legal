Stitch It! – Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 6 November 2026 · Android app, package com.stitch.it

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Stitch It! (package com.stitch.it) handles information. The Game is provided by Kristijan Isajloski. For privacy questions, contact kristijan.isajloski@gmail.com.

In short: your progress stays on your device, the Game has no accounts, sends no analytics and shows no ads.

Game progress and settings

Your puzzle progress, finished designs and settings (music, sound, haptics, language and accessibility) are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Android's own device backup may keep a copy of app data, depending on your device and Google account settings.

No accounts and no analytics

You do not sign in, and the Game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or location. The Game sends no analytics, usage statistics or crash reports of its own. Google Play may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics about installs and app stability, as it does for every app; see Google's privacy policy.

No advertising

The Game shows no ads and collects no information for advertising. Hints are free. The app package still contains an advertising library and the permissions it declares (such as the advertising ID permission), but the Game never starts that library, so it does not read your advertising ID or contact any advertising service.

Internet connection

At start-up the Game checks whether your device is online by loading a public test page. No information about you is sent with this request beyond what any internet connection reveals, such as your IP address to the server that answers it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your device, and the Game does not register with a push-notification service.

Support messages

If you contact support from the Game's Settings, your email app opens with a draft message that contains the Game's version and your device model, and a copy of your save data if you leave that option switched on. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. We then receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to answer you and fix problems. You can ask us to delete your support messages at any time.

Permissions

The Game uses internet and network-state access for the connection check, and vibration for haptic feedback. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

Deleting your data

To delete your progress and settings, open Android Settings → Apps → Stitch It! → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the Game. This permanently removes your local progress. There is no account to delete.

Children

The Game does not ask for anyone's age and collects no personal information while you play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you (in practice, only support emails), and to complain to your data-protection authority. To make a request, email us.

Changes

We will update this policy when the Game or its data practices change. The current version will always be on this page with its date.
